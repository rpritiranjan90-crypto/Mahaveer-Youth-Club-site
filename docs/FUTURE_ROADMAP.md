# 🚀 Mahaveer Youth Club Banza — Future Roadmap & Product Evolution Plan
**Strategic Vision Document (2026 – 2027+)**  
**Project:** Mahaveer Youth Club Banza Portal  

---

## 🌟 Vision Statement

To establish the **Mahaveer Youth Club Banza Portal** as the premier grassroots community platform in Odisha—uniting village youth and devotees through cultural heritage, devotional celebrations, social welfare drives, and transparent community service.

## 📌 Phase 1: Near-Term (Next 1–3 Months) — Launch & Community Rollout

### 1.1 Complete Live Deployment
- [ ] Connect custom domain (e.g. `mahaveeryouthclub.org` or `.in`).
- [ ] Establish automated daily backups with offsite synchronization.
- [ ] Set up free uptime monitoring (e.g., UptimeRobot / BetterStack pinging `/api/v1/health`).

### 1.2 Content Enrichment
- [ ] Populate complete 2012–2026 Ganesh Puja photo galleries with high-resolution memories.
- [ ] Add executive committee & active youth members with official designations.
- [ ] Publish upcoming festival schedules and meeting circulars.

### 1.3 Physical & Digital Outreach
- [ ] Generate print-ready official QR code posters for the Banza Puja Mandap counter.
- [ ] Add official website link to club's Instagram bio and YouTube channel banner.
- [ ] Broadcast official launch announcement to village WhatsApp groups.

---

## ⚡ Phase 2: Mid-Term (3–6 Months) — Experience & Engagement (V2.1 - V2.5)

### 2.1 Progressive Web App (PWA)
- Convert the website into an installable mobile app for Android and iPhone without App Store fees.
- Offline access for critical club contacts, emergency directions, and festival timings.

### 2.2 Festival Live Darshan & Aarti Stream Embed
- Integrated YouTube Live stream player directly on the homepage during annual Ganesh Puja rituals, bhajans, and immersion processions.
- Live devotional countdown timer leading up to Ganesh Chaturthi.

### 2.3 Emergency Blood Donor Directory (Social Seva)
- Voluntary blood group directory of village youth members (with opt-in privacy controls) to assist local families in medical emergencies across Jajpur district.

### 2.4 Social Media Auto-Share Preview
- Automatic generation of dynamic Open Graph banners when circulars and gallery albums are shared on WhatsApp and Facebook.

---

## 🏛️ Phase 3: Long-Term (6–12 Months) — 15-Year Anniversary Edition (V3.0)

### 3.1 15-Year Chronicle Archive (2012 – 2027)
- Commemorative interactive digital chronicle celebrating 15 years of Mahaveer Youth Club Banza.
- Senior founder interviews, audio memories, and historic photo restorations.

### 3.2 Youth Sports & Competition Portal
- Online registration and live tournament scores for annual village sports (Cricket, Volleyball, Badminton, Kabaddi).
- Student drawing and quiz competition result portal with downloadable participation certificates.

### 3.3 Role-Based Admin Access (RBAC)
- **Super Administrator:** Full system configuration, backup triggers, and credentials management.
- **Content Editor:** Drafts updates, uploads gallery photos, and prepares activity posts for super-admin approval.

### 3.4 Annual Transparency & Seva Report
- One-click export of annual welfare activities, community feasts (Bhog distribution), and village improvement drives into downloadable PDF format.
